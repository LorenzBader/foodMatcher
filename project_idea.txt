Food matcher

My idea is to implement a Web app which helps in the decision what to eat/cook in a day. 
The users are able to manage a food pool with all the meals and recipes they have every cooked or would like to cook one day.
Here users can submit new recipes.
Then there is the voting segment, which takes 10 recipes of the food pool randomly everyday and lets the user order them from 
most to least favourite.
The Overview sub-page then displays the outcome of all votings and hopefully sets a middle ground on what to cook this day.
Also, the votes of previous days can be accessed here again.
The application should have registered users to show, who voted for what and who submitted which recipe.
Furthermore, I would like to expand on my idea and implement some kind of friend system to start food votings only between
A fixed set of friends and has more than one vote at a time, if there is enough time.
Bi-directional communication could be used in a live update of the voting and the food-pool and later on in friend requests 
and friend-votings.

I would like to work on this project alone.
